Setup guide

Which device opens what

Counter tabletStaff Portal, for cashiers and baristas. Keep it signed in during the shift and switch users from the top bar.
Owner’s tablet & phoneAdmin Portal. Start or end the shift, check sales, stock and employees from anywhere.
TV or monitorQueue Screen, in full screen (F11 on a PC). It updates by itself.
Customers’ phonesMobile Menu via the table QR, and the GCash sign at the counter.

Good to know

Quick tips

  • The store opens when a shift starts. Until then the staff portal waits and the mobile menu shows the café as closed.
  • Ending the shift clocks everyone out, signs out the staff portal and clears the queue screen.
  • Voids and refunds only work for orders in the current shift, and need the cashier’s password.
  • Status dots at the top check that each app is reachable from this device. Red means that app or this device’s internet is down.
  • Add this page to the home screen of each tablet and phone (Share → Add to Home Screen) so it opens like an app.
